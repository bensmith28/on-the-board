# Civic Activist / Community Organizer

## Actions, Motivations, and Pains

### What do I do?
1. Track historical patterns in board votes, committee assignments, and official communications to build evidence-based arguments for local issues. This requires significant manual research across years of archives.
2. Organise community responses to upcoming zoning changes or budget allocations by disseminating accurate information to residents.

### Why do I do it?
1. To advocate for specific community interests (e.g., environmental conservation, fiscal responsibility).
   - related to Task 1
   - related to Task 2
2. To hold local officials accountable through data-driven evidence.
   - related to Task 1
3. To mobilize community members quickly when critical issues arise.
   - related to Task 2

### What do I want?
1. Historical Data Visibility: Easy access to past voting records and committee membership history across different boards.
2. Centralized Issue Tracking: A way to see all relevant documents (zoning, budget, etc.) grouped by topic or issue.
3. Rapid Information Dissemination: Tools or formats that make it easy to share summarized, verified information with the community.

### What's stopping me?
1. Lack of historical visibility: The difficulty of searching through years of unindexed PDFs and disparate archives makes finding past precedents extremely slow.
   - related to Want 1
2. Information Fragmentation: Having to check multiple different sources for a single issue (ZBA, Planning, Town Hall) is inefficient for organizing around a topic.
   /related to Want 2
3. Verification Burden: The time required to ensure that the information being shared matches the "Ground Truth" of official records can slow down mobilization efforts.
   - related to Want 3

## Values

### What can convince me?
The ability to present undeniable, data-backed evidence from official municipal sources that is easily verifiable by anyone in the community.

### What or who informs me?
I rely on official town archives, meeting minutes, and public notices. I am comfortable with deep-dive research using desktop tools but need quick, mobile-friendly summaries for rapid mobilization. I have a high level of research and organizational expertise.

## Context

### Where am I?
An community organizer or activist working within the municipality of Victor, NY, focused on local governance and civic engagement.

### What's my day-to-day?
My day involves researching town board activities, gathering evidence for upcoming hearings, and communicating with community members to prepare them for public participation in local government processes.

## Representative Quotes or Insights
- "We need the facts on record so that our arguments can't be dismissed."
- "If we can show a pattern of votes, we can change the conversation at the next meeting."
