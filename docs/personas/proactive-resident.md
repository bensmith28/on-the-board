# Proactive Resident

## Actions, Motivations, and Pains

### What do I do?
1. Monitor local board decisions (e.g., Planning Board, ZBA). This currently requires manually checking multiple disparate websites and reading through numerous PDFs/notices, taking several hours each week.
2. Stay updated on upcoming zoning changes and new town ordinances that might impact property values or community character.

### Why do I do it?
1. To maintain an active role in community governance.
   - related to Task 1
2. To ensure my voice is highly informed before public hearings occur.
   - related to Task 1
   - releated to Task 2

### What do I want?
1. A Unified Information Feed: All recent town activity in one central place so I don't have to visit multiple websites.
2. Standardized Summaries: Information from all sources appearing in a consistent format for quick scanning.
3. Direct Links to "Ground Truth": The ability to click through to the original official source (e.g., a PDF) for immediate verification.

### What's stopping me?
1. Fragmented information sources: Official records are spread across unindexed, disparate platforms like CivicPlus, YouTube, and physical notices. This makes it nearly impossible to maintain a cohesive understanding without significant manual effort.
   - Want 1
2. High cognitive burden: Sifting through large PDFs and various websites is time-consuming and mentally exhausting.
   - Want 1
    - Want 2
3. Lack of easy access to verified sources: It is difficult to quickly transition from a summary to the authentic legal record.
   - Want 3

## Values

### What can convince me?
The presence of direct links to official documents that allow for immediate verification of any claim made in a summary.

### What or who informs me?
I trust official municipal sources like the CivicPlus Agenda Center, YouTube recordings of meetings, and published meeting minutes/resolutions.
I prefer consuming quick updates via mobile scans but will perform deep-dive reading on my desktop when an issue is significant.
I have a moderate level of technical expertise.

## Context

### Where am I?
A resident living in Victor, NY, deeply invested in the local community and its governance.

### What's my day-to-day?
I have a busy schedule with work and family, making it impossible to attend every board meeting or manually check various town websites every day. I rely on quick, digestible updates that I can check on my phone during breaks or in the evening to stay informed about what is happening in my town.

## Representative Quotes or Insights
- "I want to know what's happening in Victor without spending my entire Sunday reading PDFs."
- "If I see a change in the feed, I need to be able to click once and see the actual resolution."
