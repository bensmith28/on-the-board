# Local Developer/Real Estate Professional

## Actions, Motivations, and Pains

### What do I do?
1. Monitor Planning Board and ZBA activities (e.g., land use applications, site plans). This requires manually tracking multiple disparate sources and reviewing detailed PDFs, which is time-consuming during busy periods.
2. Track upcoming public hearing notices to prepare for impact assessments or project opportunities.

### Why do I do it?
1. To identify new development opportunities in the area.
   - related to Task 1
   - related to Task 2
2. To assess potential impacts on existing properties and projects.
   - related to Task 1
3. To ensure timely compliance with local zoning and land use regulations.
   - related to Task 1

### What do I want?
1. Timely, Structured Updates: A single source for all recent Planning/ZBA activity in a standardized format.
2. Easy Access to Official Records: Direct links from the summary straight to the original application or site plan PDF.
3. Proactive Notifications: Alerts when new applications or hearing dates are posted so I don't miss critical deadlines.

### What's stopping me?
1. Fragmented information sources: Having to check CivicPlus, YouTube, and physical notices separately is inefficient and makes it easy to miss something.
   - related to Want 1
2. High manual effort: The time required to manually parse through unindexed websites and large PDF documents is a significant drain on productivity.
   - related to Want 1
   - related to Want 3
3. Lack of visibility into the "Ground Truth": Difficult to quickly verify details without digging through multiple layers of documentation.
   - related to Want 2

## Values

### What can convince me?
The ability to trust that the information provided is a direct and accurate reflection of the official town records, backed by immediate access to the original source documents.

### What or who informs me?
I rely on official municipal filings, meeting minutes, and legal notices from the Town of Victor. I prefer consuming professional, structured data via desktop applications or email alerts that integrate into my workflow. I have a high level of technical/professional expertise.

## Context

### Where am I?
A real estate professional or developer operating primarily within the municipality of Victor, NY, and surrounding areas.

### What's my day-to-day?
My day involves managing various property portfolios, navigating zoning regulations, and preparing for public hearings. I need information that is easy to digest and act upon without having to perform extensive manual research every time a new project arises in the area.

## Representative Quotes or Insights
- "I can't afford to miss a ZBA hearing because an application was buried in a PDF archive."
