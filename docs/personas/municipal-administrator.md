# Municipal Administrator (Secondary/System Stakeholder)

## Actions, Motivations, and Pains

### What do I do?
1. Manage the official archiving and public presentation of town records, including meeting minutes, resolutions, and notices. This currently involves manual updates to various platforms to ensure information is accessible.
2. Respond to frequent constituent inquiries regarding recent board decisions, zoning changes, and upcoming meeting schedules.

### Why do I do it?
1. To fulfill transparency and public record-keeping obligations for the Town of Victor.
   - related to Task 1
2. To reduce the administrative burden caused by repetitive manual information requests from residents.
   - related to Task 2
3. To maintain an organized and up-to-date digital presence for the municipality.
   - related to Task 1

### What do I want?
1. Automated Data Archiving: A system that automatically pulls and organizes meeting minutes and resolutions into a structured format.
2. Reduction in Manual Inquiries: A decrease in the number of phone calls and emails asking for basic information about recent board actions.
3. Standardized Presentation Template: A consistent, easy-to-maintain way to present all town activity to the public.

### What's stopping me?
1. High manual effort/overhead: The current process of manually updating disparate websites and archives is labor-intensive and prone to delays.
   - related to Want 1
2. Persistent volume of inquiries: Residents still frequently contact staff for information that exists but is difficult for them to find.
   - related to Want 2
3. Lack of a unified, automated system: The absence of an integrated pipeline means information remains fragmented across different platforms.
   - related to Want 1
   - related to Want 3

## Values

### What can convince me?
The measurable reduction in staff time spent on manual data entry and public inquiries, alongside the increased accuracy and accessibility of municipal records.

### What or who informs me?
I rely on internal town processes, official record-keeping protocols, and feedback from residents and other municipal staff. I prefer efficient, automated systems that integrate with our existing digital infrastructure and reduce need for manual intervention. I have high professional expertise in municipal administration.

## Context

### Where am I?
An administrator or clerk working within the municipal government of Victor, NY.

### What's my day-to-day?
My day involves overseeing administrative workflows, ensuring compliance with transparency laws, managing public records, and responding to the needs of both town officials and community members.

## Representative Quotes or Insights
- "We need a way to make this information available so we don't spend all day answering the same questions."
- "Automation should help us maintain accurate records without adding more work to our already busy staff."
