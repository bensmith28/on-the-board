# Local Journalist / Community Blogger

## Actions, Motivations, and Pains

### What do I do?
1. Fact-check local stories by finding official meeting minutes, resolutions, and press releases. This involves searching through multiple unindexed websites and manually parsing large PDFs, which is a significant drain on time during breaking news cycles.
2. Provide timely updates to the community regarding town board decisions and upcoming public hearings.

### Why do I do it?
1. To ensure accuracy and maintain credibility in local reporting.
   - related to Task 1
2. To keep the community informed about important local governance developments.
   - related to Task 2
3. To provide a central, easy-to-access source of "Ground Truth" information for readers.
   - related to Task 1

### What do I want?
1. Rapid Information Retrieval: A quick way to find specific facts (e.g., vote counts, resolution numbers) across all municipal sources.
2. Verified "Ground Truth" Links: Direct access to the original official PDFs and recordings to verify every claim made in an article.
3. Centralized News Feed: A single stream of updates from various boards (Planning, ZBA, etc.) so I don't have to monitor multiple platforms individually.

### What's stopping me?
1. Fragmented information sources: The need to manually check CivicPlus, YouTube, and physical notices separately is inefficient during time-sensitive reporting.
   - related to Want 3
2. High cognitive load from document parsing: Sifting through massive, unindexed PDFs is slow and prone to error.
     - related to Want 1
3. Difficulty in verifying sources quickly: It is hard to transition from a news tip to the actual official evidence without significant manual research.
   - related to Want 2

## Values

### What can convince me?
The availability of direct, unalterable links to the original town records that I can use as primary sources for my reporting.

### What or who informs me?
I trust official municipal communications, meeting recordings on YouTube, and published legal notices from the Town of Victor. I prefer a desktop-centric workflow for deep-dive research but rely on quick mobile alerts for breaking news. I have a high level of journalistic expertise.

## Context

### Where am I?
A local journalist or blogger covering news and community events within/around the municipality of Victor, NY.

### What's my day-to-day?
My day involves monitoring various communication channels for potential story leads, conducting deep-dive research into town board activities, and writing articles that inform our local audience about the decisions shaping our community.

## Representative Quotes or Insights
- "I need to be able to find the exact vote count in seconds, not minutes."
- "My readers trust me because I point them directly to the official source."
